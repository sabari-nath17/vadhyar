## Status vocabulary

1. Implemented and reproduced: name exactly what was run, by whom, against which source/environment. A service/unit reproduction does not establish mounted UI, live AI or human learning.
2. Implemented but not independently verified: inspected wiring/code or an older receipt; current-artifact execution remains open.
3. Partial or broken: a specific missing subflow or reproduced failure; distinguish observed failure from source-level concern.
4. Proposed: a specification or task with no accepted current implementation.
5. Out of scope: intentionally excluded from this milestone; no availability claim.
