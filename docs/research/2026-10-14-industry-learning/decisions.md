# Vadhyar: evidence to product decisions

14 October 2026. Codex-assisted synthesis of [industry](industry.md), [campus/learning](campus-learning.md) and [alternatives](alternatives.md), followed by a separate native skeptical review. This is a product hypothesis and mock design, not customer validation. No participant, institution, employer or human reviewer has approved the product or measured its effects.

## What did not survive the roast

- **A literature pile is not customer discovery.** Surveys establish reported problems, not a buyer for this particular product. Existing editor/tutor workflows may already be enough.
- **Six steps can become homework about homework.** Prediction and reflection must earn their time cost. E3 provides a direct warning: more engagement did not establish better post-test performance in those interfaces.
- **A green checkmark does not make an engineer.** The mock checks three prepared cases; selecting a condition is not independent code construction. Neither an export nor a separate answer proves authorship or competence.
- **“Campus to industry” is too broad for one first test.** Selected school, overseas university and experienced-programmer studies do not describe one interchangeable learner. Start with a specific prerequisite level and mentor task.
- **A polished tutor is easy to duplicate.** CodeGrade, Cursor and the research authors' own interfaces overlap. The hypothesis to test is the value of the retained decision/evidence workflow, not an assumed feature moat.

The best version below narrows the promise, preserves the evidence, and makes the simpler worksheet a credible alternative. The roast changes scope and evaluation criteria; it does not replace mixed evidence with a new confident story.

## Revised product direction

**A project reasoning and verification companion: explain one consequential code change and keep the evidence behind it.**

Choose **campus project builders who already know basic JavaScript/TypeScript and have built a small feature with AI** for the first evaluation. Their task is to explain and change a permission or state rule before a teacher/mentor discussion. Developers and engineering mentors remain a secondary workflow to investigate; they are not assumed to share the same needs or learning baseline.

This preserves Vadhyar's project-based learning goal. Industry relevance comes from the task—requirements, code context, review, debugging, edge cases and communication—rather than an “industry-ready” badge. The first prepared case is ownership in NovaDesk: a client role is insufficient to decide which client can approve a milestone. All actors and source checks remain simulated.

The need is plausible: developers report verification practices and context gaps, while onboarding includes business goals and people as well as code. Institutions ask students to follow course rules, inspect outputs and explain their work. These observations do **not** establish that people need a new tool, that they will pay, or that Vadhyar improves outcomes. [Stack Overflow 2026 verification](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai/data), [project context](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/knowledge/data), [onboarding study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05055), [Oxford guidance](https://www.ox.ac.uk/students/life/it/genai-tools).

| I04–I07: productivity/maintainability effects vary; positive, negative and null findings | Position as a companion to AI-assisted work. No universal AI-harm story or speed promise | Which task/user combination benefits enough to justify another interface? |
| I08: onboarding combines code, business context and teammates | Start from a consequential requirement; export a discussion record | Does a mentor find the record useful, and at what review cost? |

## UI and interaction contract

The mock has Today, Learn, Practice, Project, Progress and Notebook, plus a public Website view. Its warm, calm presentation is intended to make a code investigation approachable without hiding the evidence.

- **Today:** one dominant prepared lesson, the six-stage loop and a record reflecting actual actions. No invented usage, streaks or scores.
- **Learn:** one available prepared lesson. Confirm the intention, inspect source, record a prediction before observing, select an ownership condition, inspect failed/passed mock cases, reflect and answer a separate archived-state question. Labels disclose the intentionally seeded bug.
- **Practice:** two separate short questions, with explanation revealed after recording. No hints in this view; outside assistance is not observed or controlled.
- **Project:** one prepared excerpt and illustrative proposed condition. Simulating a later revision preserves the old prediction context and blocks its continuation.
- **Progress:** actual records, hints and mock check scope. Earlier attempts remain historical; a case result never certifies safety, authorship or general competence.
- **Notebook:** personal notes, reflections, search and learner-controlled Markdown export with the original source/question/rule, assistance and separate answer. Export is material for a discussion, not a verified assessment.
- **Website:** a real kind of permission decision, an honest prepared example and truthful availability. No testimonials, institutional logos, pricing, partnerships or learning-gain claims.

Prediction records retain the immutable question, input, rule and prepared source text. Selecting a condition does not write repository code. Browser checks evaluate only three active-milestone fixture cases. The archived-state answer is a separate prepared response; this mock does not execute an independent learner-authored archived guard. Guided and developer choices alter presentation, not the validity of the evidence.

## First evaluation, before a broader rebuild

1. **Problem discovery:** speak to students, computing teachers and engineering mentors about a recent AI-assisted feature, the decision they could not explain, the tools they used and what review actually required. Ask for concrete work examples before asking whether they like Vadhyar. No interviews have been conducted.
2. **Active comparator:** use the same task, source, checks and permitted assistance in the learner's existing editor with a short mentor worksheet. Compare this with Vadhyar's structured workflow. Content and assistance must be matched; avoid attributing different content quality to the interface.
3. **Primary outcome:** on a new consequential condition, assess whether the learner can identify the missing rule, make a correct change without help under declared conditions, and explain the cases. Predefine the rubric, exclusions and analysis; have reviewers score the actual work without the app's completion indicators. Determine sample size from the intended effect and feasible design rather than inventing a number here.
4. **Burden and mechanism:** record learner time, abandonment, hints/external assistance, confidence and mentor review time separately. A later unfamiliar task is needed before discussing retention or broad transfer. Course permission, participant consent and appropriate institutional determinations belong to the actual pilot.
5. **Decision:** if the worksheet performs similarly with less burden, simplify or remove compulsory steps. If benefit appears only with a mentor or for one prerequisite level, narrow the product. Do not convert activity metrics or positive usability feedback into learning efficacy or willingness to pay.

Campus and employed-junior evaluations should remain separate. A teacher's course policy is not a national rule; an employer's coding round is not evidence that this record influences hiring. India-specific evidence currently comprises limited institution documents and employer signals, not representative campus experiments, Kerala buyer discovery or procurement demand.

## Next implementation boundary

The [product logic contract](../../../prototype/logic-contract.md) records each prerequisite/output, the portable source/runner candidates and exact real-browser acceptance. A separate source-level feasibility review found the sequence implementable within that bounded scope. It also tightened restored-state prerequisites, clarified that optional hints are only part of scaffolding, and bounded the simulated later change to one distinct prepared revision. No real service or learning claim is promoted from this review.

After mock usability and discovery, the smallest real browser slice remains one trusted JS/TS repository and one confirmed consequential requirement. Reuse the existing project snapshots/check patterns where portable, with an explicitly scoped local service. Source freshness, unsaved-buffer limits, consent, authentication, fixed check execution and error/stale states require their own design and acceptance. The current mock has no such authority.

Defer LMS administration, automated grading, certificates, placement matching, adaptive curriculum, whole-repository graphs, multi-language execution and organization analytics. Those features cannot resolve the unanswered core-value question. Pricing, a business model and procurement assumptions require buyer evidence.

## Independent challenge and disposition

The skeptical reviewer found no critical flaw blocking a bounded mock, but required changes: added an active-baseline question; selected one first-evaluation audience; made learner/mentor burden a decision criterion; corrected E3's 42-recruited versus N=30 timing-test ambiguity; added the authors' public implementation as a close precedent; replaced safety/verified-independence language with scoped, self-recorded evidence. All are incorporated in this document and the exports. The strongest opposing hypothesis remains credible: Vadhyar may add paperwork without improving the learner's next unaided decision.

Review covered selected original support passages, not every source or a formal risk-of-bias audit. Publication access, English/web discovery, vendor/sampling concentration and limited Indian empirical evidence constrain the conclusions. No systematic-review, novelty, validation or institution-approval certificate is implied.
