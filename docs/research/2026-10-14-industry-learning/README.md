# Industry and campus evidence for the Vadhyar mock

Research date: 14 October 2026. This is a targeted product evidence review, not a systematic review or a market validation study. Codex performed the searches, passage checks and synthesis; no human review or user-study result is implied.

## Decision question

Which demonstrated industry and educational needs should shape a browser prototype that helps a learner reason about a bounded AI-assisted code change and inspect scoped checks?

1. Which workplace problems concern comprehension, review, debugging and onboarding? Where does AI help, hurt or have uncertain effects?
2. What do programming learning studies and institutional policies imply about assistance, independent work and assessment?
3. Which existing tools already address these jobs, and what distinct workflow can Vadhyar test?
4. Which observations justify prototype decisions, and which still require interviews, pilots or production engineering?
5. Does this workflow help the selected learner more than the same task, source, tests and assistance delivered in their existing editor plus a short mentor worksheet?

## Method and boundaries

Search original developer/employer surveys, controlled workplace and programming studies, author/publisher papers, university guidance and official product documentation. Recent AI evidence is prioritized (2023–2026), with earlier learning studies included when directly applicable. Include accessible support passages with date, population, outcome and limitations. Exclude secondhand headline statistics, inaccessible full-text claims, unsupported employability claims and vendor claims of learning efficacy. English-language and web discovery limits apply; this does not exhaust all scholarship or Indian institutions.

Industry and campus investigations run as independent native research tasks, with synthesis and comparison in the parent. Two researcher dispatches requested GPT-6 Astra; these are separate research roles, not cross-family validation. No external model upload, participant recruitment or institution approval occurred. Source identifiers are deduplicated by title/URL/version; no bibliographic API verification or human-read attestation is claimed.

The existing prepared NovaDesk ownership case remains the build slice. Research may change the purpose, copy, assistance, record and acceptance criteria. It does not authorize an LMS, grading system, employer certification or arbitrary repository execution.

## Initial challenge to the question

AI adoption alone does not demonstrate demand for Vadhyar. A survey complaint does not establish a causal learning deficit. A tested guard is not evidence of general engineering competence. A local prototype must distinguish predicted behavior, a prepared browser result, a learner-selected change and a later independent answer. Any claim that this product improves learning, onboarding speed, hiring outcomes or security remains unmeasured.
